## Findings

1. **Executor choice dominates cost.** deepseek-v4-1-flash-1017 cuts total input tokens ~48% (39.5k vs 76.7k mean) and worker output ~40% (1.7k vs 2.9k) vs glm-5-3-flash, while raising or matching pass rate. The savings come from the executor pulling less context.
2. **Planner choice dominates latency.** swe-2 lead p50 43-50s vs glm-5-3 lead 78-134s — glm is 2-3× slower on the same tasks, with 3 TIMEOUTs at the 480s cap.
3. **Best pair**: glm-5-3 → dsv41 hits 98% at 39.3k tokens; swe-2 → dsv41 hits 95% at 39.5k tokens with half the latency. Quality-equivalent, swe-2 lead is the faster choice.
4. **Worst pair**: glm-5-3 → glm-5-3-flash — 86%, 134s p50, and the most expensive (79.4k totalIn).
5. QA tasks correctly never delegate (workerOut=0 on T1-3 across all arms); delegation cost shows only on file/hard tasks.
6. Real failure modes: glm-5-3 → TIMEOUTs (3×480s), R10 (MODULES.md hard repo synthesis) failed on 3 of 4 arms at least once, blocked turn/end on 2 runs (host workflow gate, not plugin).
